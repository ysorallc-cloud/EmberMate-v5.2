How to access and export your EmberMate health data

← Back to Privacy Policy
Your data is already in your hands. EmberMate stores everything locally on your device. There is no server-side copy of your data. You can export it at any time directly from the app.

Export Your Data

EmberMate provides built-in data export in two formats:

JSON Export (Complete Data)

  1. Open EmberMate on your device
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Tap Backup & Restore
  4. Tap Export Backup
  5. Choose where to save or share the file

This creates a complete, encrypted backup of all your health data in JSON format.

Daily Care Report (Shareable Summary)

  1. Open EmberMate on your device
  2. Go to Settings > Data & Privacy
  3. Tap Export daily report
  4. Share with your care team via email, print, or messaging

This creates a human-readable summary suitable for sharing with healthcare providers.

Why We Cannot Provide Your Data

Unlike most apps, EmberMate does not store your health data on our servers. This means:

  1. We do not have a copy of your data
  2. We cannot retrieve data on your behalf
  3. We cannot provide data in response to a CCPA/GDPR subject access request, because we do not possess it

This is by design. Your privacy is protected because your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.

Lost Your Data?

If you have lost access to your data (for example, after switching devices without exporting first), we are unable to recover it. We recommend regular backups using the Export Backup feature.

Need Help?

If you have questions about accessing your data or need assistance with the export process, contact us at support@ysorallc.com.
