🗑️ Data Deletion

How to delete your health data from EmberMate

← Back to Privacy Policy
EmberMate stores all data locally on your device. There is no server-side data to delete. You have full control over your data at all times.

Delete All Data From the App

To permanently delete all your health data from EmberMate:

  1. Open EmberMate on your device
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Tap Data & Privacy
  4. Scroll to Delete My Data
  5. Tap Delete All My Data
  6. Confirm deletion when prompted (requires two confirmations for safety)
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data first using the Export feature in Data & Privacy settings.

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your data removes all of the following from your device:

  1. Medications, dosages, and medication logs
  2. Appointments and calendar entries
  3. Vital signs, symptoms, and mood logs
  4. Care plans and care team information
  5. Notes, photos, and journal entries
  6. All app preferences and settings

What Is NOT Affected

Since EmberMate does not store data on external servers, there is no server-side data to delete. Specifically:

  1. No cloud accounts to close
  2. No server databases to purge
  3. No third-party services holding your data

Crash reports sent via Sentry (if enabled) contain no personally identifiable health information and are automatically purged after 90 days.

Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling EmberMate from your device also removes all stored data, as everything is kept in the app's local storage. No residual data remains on your device after uninstallation.
